What you’re buying

A personal website made from your photos, words and memories, hosted at a private link, that you can give to someone you love. You can build it yourself with our builder, or ask us to build it for you as a custom build.

Each finished site includes unlimited pictures, a permanent link that doesn’t expire, and site maintenance, as described on our order page. We aim to deliver a finished site within 24 hours of payment and of receiving everything we need from you.

Price and payment

Our launch price is 50 GHC for our first 15 customers, against a regular price of 200 GHC. It’s a one-time payment. We arrange payment with you on WhatsApp. Your site goes live once payment is confirmed.

Building and previewing your site is free. Nothing is published until you pay.

Preview before you pay

You can preview your site in full before paying. If you don’t pay within 48 hours, we delete your draft and you’d need to start again.
