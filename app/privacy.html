Who we are

Chasing Her makes private, personal websites that people give to someone they love. In this policy, “we” and “us” means Chasing Her.

You give us personal information when you start an order, build a site, or message us on WhatsApp. This page explains what happens to it.

What we collect

  • When you start an order: your name and WhatsApp number. We also work out your country from your number’s code.
  • When you build a site: the photos, captions, letters and messages you write, song links, voice notes and music files you upload, and the unlock PIN you choose. Your work saves automatically as a draft so you can come back to it with a private resume link.
  • When you message us on WhatsApp: whatever you send us, including anything you share for a custom build.
  • Technical data: when you use our pages, your device sends us standard information such as your IP address and browser type. Our order and thank-you pages use the Meta (Facebook) Pixel to measure how our ads perform, which can involve cookies. Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts.

We do not ask for payment card details on our pages. Payment is arranged with us on WhatsApp.

How we use it

  • To build, host and deliver your site, and to contact you about your order.
  • To save your draft and let you resume it.
  • To fix problems, prevent abuse, and improve our pages.
  • To measure and improve our advertising.

We do not sell your personal information.

Who else sees it

We use a small number of services to run Chasing Her, and your information passes through them:

  • Supabase stores order details, drafts, uploaded files and finished sites.
  • GitHub may host finished sites.
  • WhatsApp (Meta) carries our conversations with you.
  • Meta and Google receive technical data from our pages as described above.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

What the person you make it for sees

A finished site is private but not secret: anyone who has both the link and your unlock PIN can open it. Please only share it with the person you built it for. Chasing Her sites record the time a site is opened, but not who opened it. Anything you put on the site, including photos of other people, will be visible to whoever you give the link and PIN to, so only include content you have the right to share.

How long we keep it

  • Unpaid drafts are deleted if the order isn’t paid within 48 hours.
  • Paid sites stay online for as long as the link is active. We keep order records as long as needed to support you and meet legal obligations.
  • Order-page details (name and WhatsApp number) are kept so we can follow up on your order.

You can ask us to delete your information at any time (see below).

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, data protection law (for example in Ghana, Nigeria and Kenya) gives you rights over your personal information, including the right to ask what we hold about you, to correct it, and to have it deleted. To use any of these rights, message us and we’ll help. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can also contact your country’s data protection authority.

You can turn off advertising cookies in your browser settings or with a tracker-blocking extension.
